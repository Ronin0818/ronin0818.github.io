Research研究

I am a master's student at Central South University, advised by Liqiang Jiang and Fengqi Guo. My work concerns the post-earthquake repair and recovery of high-speed railway bridges.我是中南大学硕士研究生，师从江力强老师和郭风琪老师，研究高铁桥梁的震后修复与性能恢复。

I use experiments and nonlinear finite-element analysis to evaluate CFRP repair of damaged bridge piers. I also develop interpretable machine-learning models to predict repaired seismic performance and assess how repair decisions affect the bridge–track system.我通过试验与非线性有限元分析评价 CFRP 对震损桥墩的修复效果，并建立可解释机器学习模型预测修复后的抗震性能，研究修复决策对桥轨系统的影响。
