What I study研究方向

How can CFRP repair return earthquake-damaged high-speed railway bridge piers to service?CFRP 修复能让震损高铁桥墩恢复到什么程度？

I use experiments and nonlinear finite-element models to measure the seismic performance that repaired piers recover. I then examine how those repairs affect the coupled bridge and track.我通过试验与非线性有限元模型，衡量修复桥墩可恢复的抗震性能，并进一步研究修复对桥梁和轨道耦合系统的影响。

I also develop interpretable machine-learning models for fast prediction across several bridge-pier performance indicators.我也建立可解释机器学习模型，用于快速预测桥墩修复后的多项性能指标。
