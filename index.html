About me关于我

I'm a master's student in the School of Civil Engineering at Central South University, advised by Liqiang Jiang and Fengqi Guo.我目前在中南大学土木工程学院攻读土木水利硕士学位，师从江力强老师和郭风琪老师。

I study the repair of earthquake-damaged high-speed railway bridges, particularly CFRP repair of bridge piers. I use experiments and nonlinear finite-element analysis to examine how much seismic performance the piers recover and how the repairs affect the bridge and track. I also work with interpretable machine-learning models to predict the performance of repaired piers.我主要研究高铁桥梁的震后修复，尤其是 CFRP 对震损桥墩的修复效果。我通过试验和非线性有限元分析，考察桥墩的抗震性能能恢复到什么程度，以及修复对桥梁和轨道的影响，也用可解释机器学习模型预测修复后的桥墩性能。
