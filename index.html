About

关于

I’m a master’s student in the School of Civil Engineering at Central South University, advised by Liqiang Jiang and Fengqi Guo. I study CFRP repair of earthquake-damaged high-speed railway bridge piers — experiments and nonlinear finite-element analysis to quantify seismic recovery and its effect on the bridge and track, and interpretable machine-learning models to predict it.

中南大学土木工程学院硕士研究生，师从江力强、郭风琪老师。研究高铁桥梁震损桥墩的 CFRP 修复：以试验与非线性有限元量化抗震性能的恢复及其对桥梁、轨道的影响，并用可解释机器学习模型预测修复效果。
