01 / ABOUT

I study earthquake-damaged high-speed railway bridges, with a focus on CFRP repair of round-end hollow piers.我研究高铁桥梁震后修复，重点关注圆端空心桥墩的 CFRP 加固。

I am a master's student at Liqiang Jiang and Fengqi Guo's research group at Central South University.我是中南大学江力强老师和郭风琪老师课题组的硕士研究生。

My work tests how much seismic performance repaired piers recover, then uses interpretable machine learning and bridge–track analysis to connect those component effects to system-level recovery.我通过试验评估修复桥墩可恢复的抗震性能，并结合可解释机器学习与桥轨系统分析，将构件修复效果与系统级恢复联系起来。
